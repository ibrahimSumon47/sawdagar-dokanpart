## Imagery and data

The catalog and gallery use original SVG patterns instead of stock photography. All 12 departments, 72 leaf categories, 24 vendor names, 432 product titles, descriptions and variant names in the seed were written for this demo, in English and Bangla. Ratings, review counts and follower counts are fabricated display summaries, not real customer reviews. Demo mobile numbers such as `01712345678` are fabricated examples in the public Bangladeshi format; they are not real customers. Record the author, source, licence and attribution requirements before adding any third-party imagery, fonts or datasets. The planned full seed sources are described in the [data specification](spec/04-data-and-seed.md); they have not been imported.
 Third-party software and its licences are listed in [technology versions](TECH_VERSIONS.md).
