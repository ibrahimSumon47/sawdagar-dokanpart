# 04 Data, seed and content sources

## Principles
Data must feel real and Bangladeshi. A deterministic seed (fixed random seed) is run only through GitHub Actions (`db-seed`, `workflow_dispatch`) via `sd seed`, and is idempotent. A nightly `demo-reset` restores demo data. No lorem ipsum, ever.

## Volumes
40+ vendors, 800+ products (about 3,000 variants), 12 top-level categories and 80+ subcategories, 300+ buyers, 1,500+ orders across all statuses over 90 days (with spikes around Eid, Pohela Boishakh and 11.11), 2,000+ reviews (about 20% with photos), 400+ Q&A threads, coupons, payouts, disputes, return requests, 30 to 60 days of analytics history, CMS content (homepage sections, banners, 6 static pages, 25+ help articles, 10+ journal posts).
Respect the free database size limit: keep rows compact and record the measured database size in `docs/DATABASE.md`.

## Where the data comes from (all free and legal)
- **Catalog curation**: write `seed/catalog/*.json` by hand-style curation: realistic Bangladeshi marketplace categories and products (Jamdani and Tangail sarees, Nakshi kantha, Rajshahi silk, Dhakai muslin scarves, jute bags and rugs, panjabi, lungi, shital pati mats, cane and bamboo crafts, terracotta and pottery, brassware, handloom textiles, leather goods, electronics and accessories, home and kitchen, beauty and personal care, books and stationery generic (no real book covers), sports and cricket gear, toys, groceries and packaged foods, fashion). Names in English and Bangla, believable descriptions, realistic BDT prices, attributes (material, size, color, origin district).
- **Product images**: Pexels and Unsplash free APIs (free API keys, owner adds them as GitHub secrets) and Wikimedia Commons or Openverse for items those do not cover. The seed workflow downloads, resizes (max 1000 px, WebP), uploads to Cloudinary, and stores blur placeholders. Keep roughly 1 to 3 images per product and cap the total so Cloudinary free credits are not exceeded; document the budget. Record the photographer, source and license for every image in `docs/CREDITS.md`. Never use copyrighted brand logos or product shots from commercial sites.
- **Vendor logos and banners**: generated by script as SVG monograms and patterns using the brand palette (original, no licensing issue).
- **Geography**: an open-licensed Bangladesh administrative dataset (divisions, districts, upazilas; for example the open `bangladesh-geocode` datasets on GitHub; verify the license and add attribution in `docs/CREDITS.md`). Seed as a lookup table.
- **People and text**: curated lists of Bangladeshi first and last names (Bangla and English), seeded `@faker-js/faker` for non-name fields, a hand-written pool of 300+ review sentences across sentiments (English and Bangla) combined with product-aware templates, plausible BD mobile numbers in the correct format (never real ones; use reserved test patterns if available and document them).
- **Orders and finance**: generated by a deterministic simulation that runs through the real domain services (so ledgers balance and state transitions are valid), not by raw inserts.
- **Demo accounts**: one per role with documented credentials in the README. Demo emails use a non-routable domain such as `@demo.sawdagar.test`.

## Seed pipeline requirements
`sd seed` supports `--scale small|full` (small for CI), prints a summary, verifies ledger balance and referential integrity at the end, and fails loudly on any inconsistency. Tests assert key invariants (stock never negative, ledger sums to zero, rating aggregates match review rows).
